# Protocol Deviations and Amendments: Additions Since the GitHub Copy Was Removed

The full log D1-D10 and A1-A12 survives verbatim inside LUMIERE-MASTER-HANDOFF.md Part 6 (the repository copy was deleted at the author's instruction on 2026-08-25). This file continues the numbering locally. Entries are dated and, per the log's rule, recorded BEFORE the data they govern is used.

**A2b (2026-08-25, applied).** Sanity constraint on the M_dd cubic rule: a cubic-derivative crossing below the measured onset is physically impossible and is rejected; remaining candidates at or beyond onset are used, else the +20-count backup applies. Adopted after the rule as literally written returned M_dd < onset on the trip-contaminated Harris 9e6 sweep.

**A13 (2026-08-25, proposed and applied to TN 3607).** For sources whose tunnel Reynolds number varies along a sweep (TN 3607's induction tunnel: 0.7e6 at M 0.30 to 1.6e6 at M 1.00), the subcritical baseline is NOT the A1 flat mean. It is the Reynolds-trend-corrected subcritical shape anchored to the measured level: operationally, the increment at Mach M is CD_meas(M) minus [CD_meas(M_ref) + (CD_pred(Re(M)) - CD_pred(Re(M_ref)))], with M_ref = 0.50 and the prediction evaluated at the facility's convention n_crit. A1 remains correct for constant-Re facilities (Harris, Guidonia).

**A14 (2026-08-25).** Facility n_crit convention entry for the Langley 4x19 inch semiopen tunnel: n_crit 6 (gate best satisfied there; the report itself never states transition; the surface pressure orifices are an unquantified trip risk, noted).

**A15 (2026-08-25).** TN 3607 reports normal-force coefficient cn, in a different axis system from its wake-survey drag. Predictions are converted to cn via cn = cl cos(alpha) + cd sin(alpha) before comparison; at alpha_test = 0 on symmetric sections the distinction vanishes.

**D11 (2026-08-28, pre-declared BEFORE any TN 1546 data value is read).** The one-shot family-holdout scoring will use a PRE-DECLARED EIGHT-AIRFOIL SUBSET of TN 1546, chosen for maximal geometric leverage before any of its data was seen: the thickness ladder at design lift 0.3 (16-306, 16-309, 16-312, 16-315) and the camber ladder at 9 percent thickness (16-009, 16-109, 16-209, 16-509), two low-angle sweeps each (alpha_test -0.23 and 1.77 deg). Rationale: 24 full airfoils of markerless faired curves exceed what can be extracted at QC standard in this phase; the subset spans both geometric axes the recalibration fits. The remaining 16 airfoils are declared future work, and scoring on the subset is reported as exactly that, never as "the full 16-series family." Page assignments follow the Table I ordering (16-009 = scan p21 through 16-1012 = scan p44) and every page's caption is verified before reading; a caption mismatch stops that page.

**D12 (2026-08-28).** TN 1546 wall-constriction handling: the report states its data are uncorrected for wall constriction, about 2 percent in Mach at supercritical speeds. Per its own Figure 2 example, the correction is applied at analysis time as a Mach shift only where M exceeds 0.75, linearly ramped from zero at M 0.70 to 2 percent at M 0.80 and held. This rule is fixed now, before extraction; its crudeness is carried as a +/-0.01 systematic on M_dd from this source and stated in Limitations.
